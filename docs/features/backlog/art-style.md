## Reference and intended feeling

- Primary references: [Berry Bury Berry](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3370870/Berry_Bury_Berry/) and [A Game About Digging a Hole](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3244220/A_Game_About_Digging_A_Hole/), official screenshots inspected 2026-09-12. Our interpretation follows the user's explicit bright/cartoon/simple-item request: lively colors, readable shapes and restrained surface detail. Their actual texture resolutions are not known or adopted as a specification.
- Berry supplies the bold color/shape reference; Digging supplies the tactile, readable earth/cut-face reference. This selects no horror presentation, CRT filter, outlines, content, equipment or mechanics from either game. Screenshots are reference only; no artwork is imported from them.
- Earlier [JulioVII Stylized Grass & Dirt](https://juliovii.itch.io/ftpgrass-dirt) remains a secondary painted-surface reference. Its download is not approved or used. Broad patches and material identity remain useful; fine PBR detail must yield to the user's newer cartoon direction.
- The world feels bright, playful and tangible. Excavated shapes and familiar finds carry the detail; soil must remain quiet enough that a partly exposed bottle/rock is recognizable without a detector. Common finds remain silent.

## Runtime and matching rules

- Accepted ground: six **2048×2048** Blender-baked maps, 2 m soil / 1.25 m turf tiles, Repeat, trilinear mipmaps and 16× anisotropy. R7 turf uses three world planes, so steep lips retain leaf shapes rather than top-projection streaks. Soil blending excludes axes more than 0.16 below the strongest normal component, then selects a coherent mineral sample within those facing planes. Full stone faces do not mix with unrelated dirt projections; only a narrow derivative-filtered boundary blends. Explicit world-position gradients avoid coarse-mip selection at projection-sign boundaries. Color, normal and roughness share coordinates/weights. The 0.035 m cap retains a narrow screen-filtered edge with a 1.5 mm minimum blend.
- BC7 color/surface-mask and BC5 normals remain; masks pack roughness/contact in R/G and soil stone coverage in B. Relief is 0.55 soil / 0.90 stone / 0.40 turf; macro variation 0.06. All 110 r7 stone identities, geometry and ~1.56% coverage remain. R8 changes only the soil albedo: uniform warm mineral tint replaces heavy dirt-pattern coating; pixels outside stone coverage are identical. R7 normal/mask and all r6 turf maps remain byte-identical. Six compressed mip chains remain ~32 MiB; this revision adds no texture samples or runtime stone geometry. 135 restores the turf branch across the original upper surface; its depth guard still skips turf underground. Grass retains 133's geometry/batching optimizations; earlier native measurements apply to their recorded layouts, and minimum-hardware qualification remains 54.
- R6 shadow quality: 4096px main-light atlas, four cascades at 4.5/11.7/24.75 m within the retained 45 m range, high soft-shadow filtering, explicit Sun depth/normal bias 0.05/0.12 and 0.12 cascade border. SSAO retains depth reconstruction, eight samples, 0.18 m radius and 16 m falloff but uses full resolution. This costs additional shadow storage/draw/filter work and four times the AO evaluation pixels; native minimum-hardware/performance qualification remains 54. Ambient colors and shadow settings remain; r8 changes only the distance-to-daylight curve in the existing connected-air cache.
- Start future small props at 256–512px and larger station/equipment sheets at 512–1024px; raise resolution only for a demonstrated player-distance readability need. These are authoring targets, not claims about the references. Keep mipmaps/filtering; lowering resolution does not mean forced pixelation or blurry labels.
- Check assets at shovel distance, ordinary 3 m collection reach, in shadow and against both grass and soil. Recognizable silhouette and a distinct value/color region matter more than increased saturation. Keep the [Windows budgets](release-validation.md#release-budgets); native minimum-hardware qualification remains 54.
- Future matching examples: bottle = clean glass body plus clear cream label remnants; collectible rock = several broad planes with restrained mineral variation; shovel = chunky blade/handle and recognizable hardware; station = broad amber/teal panels. These are design briefs, not approval to create new models.

## Trial verdict and ownership

- **Sunny r8 accepted.** R1/r2 were rejected for washed-out/flat detail; r3 had a thick turf lip, uniform stones and overly bright caves. R4 soil improved but its stones were too numerous. R5/R6 retained blur; r7 was rejected for rapid darkness and painted/translucent stone faces. R8 corrected the mineral blending/coating and shallow light falloff; the user accepted the result. B Soft had weaker cream-label separation and C Bold busier emerald/ochre contrast. Do not re-ask A/B/C selection. Upright moving grass is separately implemented in 127 with explicit batch approval; the underlying ground/daylight acceptance remains 105.
- Rejected directions: the old olive palette conflicts with the brighter cartoon feel; uniform dots were rejected as artificial, and r1's broad cloudy 512px surfaces were rejected as washed out. Simple cartoon style must still have clear, well-resolved authored detail. Flat untextured placeholders and blanket outlines are not acceptable substitutes for authored surfaces. Earlier requests for richer bottle wear remain compatible only when details support the broad cartoon forms.
- The guide defines the whole game's direction; 105 delivered the accepted original soil/turf and explicitly requested natural daylight behavior. Retain the user's terrain, existing props and save identities. Remaining boundary/scenery placeholders, final bottle/rock acceptance and shovel presentation belong to their existing production tasks, and are not declared complete here.
- [Asset ledger](../../asset-ledger.md#task-77---original-soil-and-short-turf) retains the original direct-creation approval, exact sources/license, owned paths and rollback. 105 reuses that soil/turf scope. New models, imported packs and audio still need specific approval.
