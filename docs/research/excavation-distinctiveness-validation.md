# Distinctiveness: measurement hypotheses

Companion to the [feedback assessment](excavation-distinctiveness.md). These are proposed starting thresholds from the supplied report, not validated benchmarks, release requirements or current-player verdicts. `148` owns the slice decision; existing playtest sheets retain observations and fixes. Record build, seed, equipment, route, participant count and unprompted behaviour; small samples are qualitative evidence.

| Question | Proposed observation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is excavation itself satisfying? | Three reward-free minutes; around 70% describe it as satisfying or voluntarily continue. Start with five target players and report counts, not false precision. | `100`, `147`, `148` |
| Does the opening create curiosity? | First recognizable noteworthy discovery around 2–3 minutes; ordinary ore income alone does not satisfy this measure. | `102`, `148`, `37` |
| Are long stretches anonymous? | Inspect gaps over roughly 4–5 minutes without a discovery, useful upgrade anticipation or meaningful environmental change. Investigate cause rather than forcing timed spawns. | `45`, `37` |
| Are lateral routes attractive? | In 15 minutes, most players voluntarily investigate away from straight down at least once; record reason/shape. A 90-degree turn is a source example, not a route quota or player failure. | `10`, `102`, `148`, `37` |
| Does a partial reveal invite finishing? | Around 80% voluntarily continue uncovering after recognition. Observe desire to reveal, not compliance with forced 100% exposure or delayed pickup. | `57`, `102`, `148` |
| Does a major purchase change play? | Player describes shape, scale, information or old-material access without opening stats. Minor capacity tiers may stay numerical. | `56`, `103`, `148` |
| Does the middle stay interesting? | At 45–60 minutes, investigate if roughly 20–25% independently call it repetitive/grindy. Short slice results cannot pass this full-run case. | `103`, `104`, `37` |
| Are finds remembered? | After 20–30 minutes, most name a specific object/event before a stat upgrade. Compare isolated finds and related clusters. | `102`, `148`, `45` |
| Does a silent clip explain the game? | Ten seconds: roughly 70% identify excavation with unusual machinery; roughly 50% mention tool or discovery unprompted. Test only truthful implemented footage. | `153` |

## Bounded experiment inputs

- Compare the user-liked ordinary Scoop with explicitly labelled admin Shave research around an actual find; further Bore/Fan comparisons are optional after their unconvincing verdict. `56`/`120` must select meaningful production progression before any mode package enters normal-game acceptance. Separate prepared experimental kit from earned normal kit in every result.
- Compare handheld → appliance → vehicle scale, with approved handling for large finds. Use five recognizable objects before broad production; `42` owns content and `148` the combined verdict.
- Observe voluntary archive browsing after the first few records; eight first discoveries is a source test size, not a new required roster/display cap. `60` decides snapshot/retention rules.
- `149` compares five uncanny variant briefs and roughly 5–10% occurrence as hypotheses; a change of tint or price alone does not prove surprise. `150` measures the selected rate and total reward budget.
- Optional suction: compare cleanup time and share of useful excavation; around one-third of total excavation is an investigation trigger, not a hard player restriction. Preserve automatic cleanup and existing RMB handling.
- `45` compares five related cluster templates with roughly ten isolated finds, using recall and voluntary lateral investigation; named objects count once in the finite roster.
- `39`/`103` observe whether players remember and revisit an unmarked hard formation after buying power. Lack of revisits can indicate unreadable material or low reward, not a need for a quest marker.
- `151` may compare a bounded 30–60-second snapshot interval against no recap; `152` may compare four automatic camp states. Both remain deferred and require selection before runtime capture/assets.

## Reporting

Separate natural earning runs from prepared equipment comparisons. Preserve uninterested/negative feedback and reasons for stopping; do not merge “said it was satisfying” with “continued” without showing both counts. Use the existing [playtest/fix workflow](../development/playtesting.md), name the owning fix, and repeat only affected cases. Do not recruit/message participants, publish footage or schedule work from this document alone.
