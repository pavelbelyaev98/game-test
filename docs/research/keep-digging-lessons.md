# Keep Digging reports: decisions for this project

## Source and confidence

Read both user-supplied reports on 2026-09-13: **Deep Research: What Keep Digging Teaches Us About Your Excavation Game** and **Keep Digging: What Its Reception Says About the Digging-Game Opportunity**. The first compares the genre and proposes progression/positioning; the second focuses on reception, version changes and technical friction. This synthesis retains their actionable content so tasks do not depend on attachments.

Their market numbers, patch chronology, review quotes and causal claims were not independently verified here. They share sources and are not two independent experiments. One report explicitly uses ten surfaced launch reviews, not a representative sample; launch and later-overhaul feedback must not be conflated. Opaque citation tokens cannot be resolved from the attachments. Do not publish their percentages, rankings, commercial scores or claimed causes as our findings. Their linked [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3585800/Keep_Digging/) and [official updates](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3585800/allnews/) are a trail for any later verification.

## User verdict overrides the proposed gun pivot

The user **really likes Shave**, finds the other new modes unconvincing, prefers the earlier digging, wants better/more distinctive progression, and explicitly requests removal of sounds. The four-mode package, its tool art/animations and tier attachment scheme are **experimental only**, not selected production features. `154` restores normal Scoop and puts the silent experiment behind admin opt-in. No new sounds may be added unless the user later explicitly changes that direction.

## Next decisions and evidence

1. Deliver `154`'s normal-game restoration, silent admin experiment and explicit feedback records.
2. Prioritize `56`: propose a small set of distinctive, earnable early/middle/late capabilities on the liked baseline. Explain each with a concrete situation, before/after outcome, tradeoff and first useful application; retain current purchases until reviewed migration exists.
3. `120` compares where Shave belongs (an evolving action, optional capability, or remaining experimental). Liking its feel is not approval of a gun silhouette, four-mode ladder or free production unlocks.
4. Resolve `117`/`96`/`97`/`40` and `155`, then test the first accepted discovery slice in `148` before broad content. Existing numerical hypotheses are exploratory, not market forecasts.

Judge the player-created hole, remembered finds, useful purchase choices, voluntary lateral movement and return overhead. The 2–3 hour concept target remains; do not extend playtime through blueprint RNG, generic ores, commuting or an anticlimactic late grind.
