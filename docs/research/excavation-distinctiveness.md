## Planning direction

Prioritize a recognizable excavation action, anticipation and memorable reveals before expanding the roster. The intended reward chain is signal/visible clue → investigation → partial reveal → recognition or surprise → useful income/persistent memory → new capability. Ordinary mineral income remains useful; `145` is an implemented baseline, not evidence that the full discovery fantasy works.

The proposed identity is memorable first-person excavation and discovery, with meaningful earned capability and increasingly surprising objects. The four-mode machine remains an experiment; its gun silhouette is not a selected identity. Keep the 2–3 hour target, free routes, saved excavation, useful late upgrades and an ending using acquired tools.

## Conflicts resolved for planning

- One evolving primary tool and current hold/toggle comfort remain. Normal digging is restored; Q cycling belongs only to the explicitly labelled admin experiment. The user’s positive Shave verdict does not approve a gun, four-mode ladder, sounds or mandatory switching.
- `145`'s 32 m site stays. The report's “car forty metres down” is an illustrative memory, not a depth increase. Large finds remain occasional; three scales are a test proposal, not a giant-object-only game or instant vehicle pickup.
- Keep 60% ordinary exposure, immediate eligible aimed pickup and existing lift/drop/throw. Compare recognition without forcing 100% exposure, extra observation time, hidden last-clump cleaning or globally removing percentages.
- Keep disconnected/tiny-remnant cleanup free and automatic. A vacuum cannot require nuisance fragments to be reintroduced or earn its usefulness by undoing completed fixes.
- No crafting, survival meters, police/neighbours, base management, combat, mandatory archaeology or puzzle campaign. These suggestions reinforce existing exclusions.
- Small playable experiments still require production integration and approved assets. Primitives may be used only in labelled validation fixtures; the report's cheap mockup advice does not lower the MainGame bar.
