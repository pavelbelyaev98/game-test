# Player feel and design risks

Current guardrails for future work, grounded in the [Steam audit](../research/steam-review-audit/report.md) and [additional review analysis](../research/steam-review-audit/player-feel.md). These are risks to test, not claims that the current build fails. Detailed behavior belongs to the linked feature/task. Existing completed work stays completed; a passing code test alone does not resolve a player-feel risk.

| Priority / risk | Intended feeling | Warning sign / what NOT to do | Owner and evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High: forgettable higher-tier discoveries | A find above common produces a stronger moment of recognition or surprise | Distinctive models are remembered only as sale values. Do not solve this with more interchangeable loot, lore homework, compulsory buffs or requiring a story from every common stone | [Discovery](../features/backlog/discovery-content.md); `40`/`42` brief higher-tier recognition/reward, `102`/`37` observe recall; `89`/`09` retain satisfying common collection |
| High: powerful digging hides the reward | I uncovered that object and understood what I picked up | Fast held digging erases the reveal; particles or notices obscure it. Do not add mandatory inspection, extra clicks or forced pauses | [Collection](../features/backlog/discovery-collection.md); `57` proposes feedback priority, `09` tests rapid/repeated pickups at strong tool tiers |
| High: misleading visual promises | The world suggests actions that actually work | Decorative doors/containers resemble usable ones, or a hard wall looks identical to a boundary. Do not make every prop interactive to compensate | [Presentation](../features/backlog/presentation-audio.md); `57`/`58` brief affordances, `08`/`15` record attempted actions and misconceptions |
| High: unseen or inconsistent UI states | I understand the current situation and available actions in every flow | Do not review only the usual screens, omit conditional/error menus, lose useful recovery choices or claim all copy is covered from a string search | [UI review](tasks/106-ui-ux-audit-design.md); `106` catalogs exact copy/conditions with visual evidence, `107` applies reviewed cleanup and user-tests affected branches |
| High: overconfident return advice | I can judge my reserve and choose whether to push on | A battery band is mistaken for an escape guarantee. Do not label unmeasured route safety as certain or add automatic route solving | [Return](../features/backlog/return-rescue.md); new `93` compares warning meanings, `94` updates existing HUD/model; observed comprehension in `15` |
| High: repeated commuting | Returning banks a successful trip and creates anticipation | Bag/energy limits alternate tiny trips; service feels empty or bureaucratic. Do not add extra meters, long station rituals or teleport incentives | [Core loop](../features/backlog/core-loop.md); `57` briefs concise acknowledgment, `15` measures the trip, `59`/`37` tune the friction budget |
| High: nominal power, no practical gain | My purchase visibly changes what I can do | Faster digging only fills the bag sooner; old ground scales up; all wanted purchases end early. Do not pad prices, force depth gates or reset tools | [Upgrades](../features/backlog/selling-upgrades.md); `56` defines benefits, `37` compares whole trips, explorers and rushers |
| High: finite-money dead end | A mistake hurts, but my excavation is still worth continuing | Reachable remaining income cannot fund necessary progress. Do not assume a successful rescue proves campaign recovery, or silently add respawns/debt/refunds | [Return](../features/backlog/return-rescue.md); `56`/`59` specify viability, `50`/`37` demonstrate recoverable loss cases |
| High: visible but unrecoverable finds | Exposing a find brings it within an achievable plan | Ceiling/boundary objects remain unreachable after normal upgrades and widening. Do not fix this with compulsory explosives or collection through soil | [Generation](tasks/45-discovery-generation.md); `45`/`47` test actual normal-tool recovery after excavation and reload |
| Medium: detector becomes a supervisor | A signal invites an investigation I may ignore; I recognize common finds myself | Do not signal any minor/common find, even metal/large/clustered ones, or bypass this with upgrades. Avoid constant pulses, price/GPS disclosure and compulsory signal chasing | [Detector](../features/backlog/detector.md); `09` authors silent minors, `10`/`49` verify exclusion/focus/comfort; `102` observes mixed-content routes |
| High: inconsistent or noisy art | The world belongs together and partly exposed objects are recognizable | Do not pick materials from attractive preview spheres alone, mix incompatible styles, camouflage loot with detail, or fix it through common-find detector cues | [Art style](../features/backlog/art-style.md); `105` compares/revises packs in-game until the user selects a tested direction; `57`/`08`/`09` carry it forward |
| Medium: item tiers become indistinguishable | Routine bottles/rocks feel good to collect; higher-tier finds create a step up | Do not equate recognizable with common, make toy cars routine filler, or demand a story from every stone. Do not compensate with crafting or detector cues | [Content](../features/backlog/discovery-content.md#item-tiers-and-frequency); `89`/`09` test truly common pickups, `40` selects higher-tier assignments/frequencies |
| High: empty personal record | My display recalls my own discoveries | Photos are buried, dark or unrecognizable; recognition appears only after a reset. Do not replace memories with completion counters or stock catalog images | [Display design](tasks/60-discovery-display-design.md); `60` selects legibility rules, `51` tests real snapshots and same-save persistence |
| High: ending breaks the promise | The strange final discovery makes the journey click | Sudden reflex/stealth test, lost equipment, unexplained local joke or abrupt cutoff. Do not use a genre switch, mandatory reset or longer grind as payoff | [Ending design](tasks/61-ending-design.md); `61`/`52` test setup, comprehension, earned power and same-save Continue |
| High: progress feels unsafe | I can stop and trust what will resume | Save hitches/notices interrupt digging, wording contradicts saving, or related state restores separately. Do not disguise uncertainty with success messages | [Persistence](../features/backlog/core-loop.md); retain `35`/`79`, deferred `80` owns performance, `91` portability, `54` full-run qualification |
| Medium: completion becomes obligation | I continue because I want another discovery | Random speed retries, a final invisible speck or destroying a favorite route for a badge. Do not turn voluntary clearing into a 100% terrain chore | [Achievements](tasks/55-achievement-design.md); `55` tests same-save attainability and playstyle cost; `84`/`85` assistance stays conditional |
| High: chasing the wrong audience | A dense, finite excavation adventure fulfills its own promise | Extra mechanics/empty depth are added to appease every negative review. Do not infer a price, sales forecast or need for combat/endless play from this corpus | [Pacing](tasks/37-full-run-pacing.md); `15` proves one appealing trip, `37` qualifies the 2–3-hour hypothesis, `54` checks the finished experience |

## State and reuse

- **Selected guardrails:** preserve the concept's free excavation, one evolving shovel, held/toggle digging, immediate identification, finite finds, ordinary physical return and same-save continuation. Review recommendations do not select assets or optional systems.
- **Proposed design work:** `93` resolves warning semantics before `94`; feedback priority/recognition timing remains a proposal in `57`. Any change to collection thresholds, delays, penalty policy, markers or completion assistance requires its existing design decision.
- **New user ideas:** [assessment](../research/player-idea-assessment.md) assigns selective motion `96`, deliberate specials/chests `97`, refill/field charges `98` and mobility/teleport `99`; sound briefs stay with `40`, dynamite with `71`. These may revisit baseline rules through concrete design decisions, not silent implementation. The horizontal-travel criticism was about Meltopia; [user playtests](playtesting.md) have no prefilled pass/failure for our game.
- **Open validation:** qualitative feel, pacing, solvency and final presentation remain unproven until their owners record playable evidence. `80` stays deferred; this research does not resume its benchmark.
- Read this index plus the active task and feature. Open the relevant research section or a selected evidence row only when its rationale is needed; do not load the whole review corpus into routine implementation context.
