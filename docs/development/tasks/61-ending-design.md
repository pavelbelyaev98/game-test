# Task 61 - Design the final discovery and ending payoff

Type: design/research; documentation only. Status: `planned`. Prerequisites: `51` and the preceding working content/equipment; consult `55` achievement goals.

Feature: [ending](../../features/backlog/ending.md). Implementation: `52`. [Queue](../tasks.md).

## Research and proposal

- Read idea sections 46–49 and [persistent-investment findings](../../research/player-review-findings.md#persistent-investment); use the actual late-game kit and discovery roster. This is the deliberately late decision about the exact final object, not an early commitment to the report's example.
- Present a few concrete payoff options and recommend one: final object, why it is impossible, how prior discoveries support it and why the protagonist becomes famous. Define the chosen player's excavation/recognition sequence before the cutscene.
- Specify the final trigger, any selected protected component's obvious/automatic use, the cutscene beats and Continue behaviour. No forced stealth, combat, puzzles, maxed tracks, rare-passive requirement or explosives stockpile.
- Evaluate the optional 3–5 short buyer reactions or surface headlines against discovery-only foreshadowing. If useful, propose exact text, triggers/order, non-blocking placement/duration and reload/Continue repeat rules; no voice, dialogue tree, required reading or routine subtitle spam. Named chushkopek/moonshine examples do not choose the final object.
- Map generation safety, selling/rescue, interruption/reload and repeated-trigger cases. Preserve the same hole, equipment, money, photos and saved completion facts; coordinate achievement conditions without making them missable.
- Produce an asset/cutscene brief and required state transitions. Do not author final art/audio or publish anything during this planning task.

## Questions to resolve with the user

Choose the final-object/payoff proposal, confirm tone and cutscene scope, and decide whether protected components are needed at all. Also review whether a few text reactions improve the buildup at all; if selected, choose buyer/headline framing, tone, spoiler limits, readability/dismissal and exact lines from a concrete proposal. These must be concrete choices, not a request for the user to write the ending from scratch.

## Done when

- The accepted narrative/interaction brief and recovery cases are recorded in the ending feature and referenced by `52`.
- Implementation has a specific payoff and trigger contract; ordinary upgraded excavation leads into it, and no remaining core narrative decision is disguised as a coding detail. Asset batches still require their own explicit approval.
