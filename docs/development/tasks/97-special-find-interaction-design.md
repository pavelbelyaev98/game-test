## Proposal to prepare

- Compare existing held pickup with a deliberate **bound Interact action, E by default**, for distinctive objects. Ordinary finds keep LMB. Show what sustained/toggled digging does when a special becomes collectible; it must neither auto-collect the special nor demand release on every ordinary item. Define priority over stations, prompts, repeat presses, pause/focus and rebinding.
- Consume `117`'s selected purpose, frequency and sale/retention categories; this task no longer chooses the overall reason for uniques or their copy policy. Build the interaction matrix for those categories, ordinary items, any selected chest and large-find interactions. Examples are illustrative, not asset/roster approval; size, rarity and interaction remain distinct.
- For each category, map the selected reward policy to recognition, action, destination/capacity, sale availability, persistence/rescue and first-discovery records. Preserve the established `62`/`36` optional passives and `61` ending ownership; do not allow selling required protected state or quietly grant ordinary objects a new rescue exemption.
- Prepare a short chest opening sequence: required exposure, E to open, reveal, reward transfer and later empty state. Compare direct rewards with individually collectible contents; solve full inventory, moving/blocked lids, saving during opening, interruption and repeat visits. Select when reward identity/content is fixed so reload does not reroll it or duplicate value.
- Give chest contents a meaningful authored reward rationale and count them in `40`/`45`'s finite budget. No paid random rolls, separate key-search loop, repeated long opening animation or container whose reward cannot justify the interaction. One or two illustrative ordinary/strange chest briefs are enough to review the mechanic.
- Show a clearly documented sale/use outcome before an irreversible choice. A distinctive object may justify itself through humor, context and the personal display; recommend against requiring a functional power or quest for every unique.

## Questions for review

Review E-for-specials versus the current held flow, which selected categories need deliberate acknowledgment, chest reward transfer and communication of `117`'s sale/retention outcomes. Reopen a purpose/reward decision through `117` if an interaction exposes a conflict; do not silently choose a different collectible economy here.
