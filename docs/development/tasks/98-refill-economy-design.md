# Task 98 - Compare refill pricing and optional field charges

Type: design/research; documentation only. Status: `planned`. Prerequisites: current-trip observations `101` and complete production trip `15`; consume `56`'s available equipment assumptions without resuming its paused work. Final numerical balance remains `37`.

Feature: [battery and jetpack](../../features/backlog/battery-jetpack.md). Context: [idea assessment](../../research/player-idea-assessment.md), [finite recovery risk](../design-risks.md). Coordinate `59` rescue and `99` travel costs. No refill implementation task exists until a policy is selected.

Priority: revisit after the complete production trip `15`, using current `101` observations as early input. Earlier equipment work may retain the free-base baseline; this optional comparison does not block `56` or authorize a recharge fee.

## Concrete options and evidence

- Record the user's preference to consider non-free refills. Compare **free base refill + optional field convenience**, **base refill priced by charge actually added**, and **an explicitly limited safe basic refill with paid extra charge**. Prepare representative early/late and poor/rich trip ledgers with sale income, refill, next purchase, rescue and any selected travel cost; label illustrative numbers, do not invent measured balance.
- State what decision a service charge creates. Compare whether it changes how far to push, creates purchase tradeoffs, or merely delays every upgrade. Initial recommendation: keep the current free-base baseline unless an alternative produces a worthwhile choice and remains easy to understand; the user may select a different policy after seeing examples.
- Compare no portable charge, a found limited-use charge, and a paid carry-limited pack. Use the same shared battery; specify restored amount/cap, pickup versus manual use, bag/carry policy, availability, full-battery handling, save/rescue and no-duplication behavior. A new inventory wheel, crafting recipe or second fuel meter is not implied.
- For paid service, specify when a transaction occurs, its visible price, exact energy delivered, insufficient funds and service near the existing sell point. Walking through today's automatic recharge zone must not silently bill repeatedly. Review quick deliberate service versus another safe transaction flow; do not assume auto-selling finds is authorized.
- Demonstrate a finishable ordinary run and recoverable mistakes with finite reachable loot. Exercise empty wallet/bag/battery, depleted nearby finds, partial fill, unaffordable full fill, repeated rescue and upgraded capacity. Emergency rescue's free refill must not make deliberate depletion the cheapest routine purchase; `59` owns any changed rescue consequence proposal.
- Produce a recommended policy, ledgers and player-facing examples. If paid running costs need debt, new loot generation, forced grinding or a hidden subsidy to work, identify that tradeoff before selecting them. None is an automatic remedy.

## Review and acceptance

Review which charge policy adds enjoyment, whether a pack is a found surprise or planned purchase, and how low-money recovery works. Prices remain tuning; charge policy, carrying/use and recovery guarantees are product choices.

Record the selected policy in the battery feature and update concept section 34 explicitly if free base recharge is replaced. Pass cost assumptions to `56`/`59`/`99`/`37`, and create a numbered implementation task only for selected changes. Preserve `13` as the completed baseline; the proposal itself neither charges money nor grants portable fuel.
