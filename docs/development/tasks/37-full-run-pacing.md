# Task 37 - Balance discovery and useful purchases through the 2–3 hour run

Type: validation. Status: `planned`. Prerequisites: 36, 52 and the full intended systems/content.

Feature: [selling upgrades](../../features/backlog/selling-upgrades.md). [Queue](../tasks.md). [Research basis](../../research/player-review-findings.md).

## Task contract

- Gap: `12` validates transactions and five combined shovel prices; neither those prices nor the 96 development finds establish a balanced 2–3 hour game. Keep `12` done. This pass follows `15`/`25`, materials/content (`39`–`45`), equipment/falls (`46`–`50`), display/ending (`51`/`52`) and passive rewards (`36`).
- Tune income, prices and useful upgrade choices across the short run so players can still afford and benefit from purchases late in the game. Do not require all tracks to be maxed for the ending or postpone every meaningful purchase until after it. Preserve money-based sequential purchases and avoid artificial depth/time gates or grind.
- Major tool upgrades must make the same previously difficult terrain genuinely easy, with useful access in downward and sideways branches. Do not immediately offset bought power with proportional terrain resistance; late purchases need enough remaining excavation to demonstrate their benefit.
- Acceptance: record representative complete runs with differing purchase priorities and layouts, including no passive rewards and an early lucky reward. Compare matched straight-down rush, signal-led lateral and mixed strategies across accepted seeds: depth alone must not dominate income/progression, and lateral interest cannot depend on forcing detours. Record ordinary-find income contribution into late play and first-trip digging/return ratios. Note time to first ending, income/spend and meaningful early/middle/late purchases. Verify that players do not exhaust all useful upgrades early, miss the 2–3 hour target through forced grinding, or need a rare reward to finish.
- Record the player's current limiting factor and reason for each purchase across early/middle/late trips. Test whether inventory, energy, resistance, mobility and searching each justify choices without a fixed upgrade order; no single track should be the automatic best purchase throughout.
- Compare rich/poor, empty/full-bag and deep/shallow physical returns against repeat rescue under `59`/`50`. Rescue cannot become an optimal routine shortcut, yet low-money recovery must remain possible. Tune the actual economy rather than assuming the current capped fee scales.
- Include unguided excavation observations from `15`: record shape, widening/route work and player intent, not just signal-to-find efficiency. Do not require a specific shaft/branch ratio or reward deleting 100% of terrain.
- Confirm remaining upgrades can still be bought and used in Continue Playing. Exact prices, track lengths and late-purchase timings remain open until these runs.
- Before implementation: read [progression/discovery findings](../../research/player-review-findings.md#progression-and-discovery) and inspect current prices, content and prior trip evidence. Research through recorded full runs: log discovery identity/first recognition, gaps between distinctive finds, income/purchases, dig/return/station time, rescue and old-obstacle revisits. Local test instrumentation is enough; no analytics service is implied.
- Ask the user to review a concrete pacing proposal only if evidence suggests changing agreed track lengths, world/content scope or return convenience. Keep the 2–3 hour target and money-only sequential progression; tune ordinary numerical prices/density from evidence. Diagnose getting lost separately from boring travel; HOME/markers consume `70` decisions and remain conditional; outposts require a separate decision.
- Acceptance also requires recognizable novelty into late play, clear purchase effects and meaningful use of late purchases before the payoff. Redistribute approved content or revise prices before proposing additional empty depth, new mechanics or forced grinding. Compare the `45` layout checks with observed discovery gaps/clumping and late novelty. Adjust spatial guardrails from route evidence; a passing seed does not guarantee a particular encounter minute. The reports' review counts are not target metrics for our game.
