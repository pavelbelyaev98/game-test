## Scope

- Design the first production batch of minor/ordinary finds to replace the current development forms. Prepare a concrete named roster for review before asking the user to choose individual objects.
- User direction: begin with **three truly common items**, such as bottles and plain rocks; interpret this as three types with repeated instances, with exact population open. A toy car is explicitly above common. The [revised proposal](../../research/starter-setting-and-items.md) recommends bottle/plain stone/crumpled can; its 20 more individual belongings are later-roster candidates, not common starters. Exact trio and drained-reservoir premise remain unselected; `58` retains full site/material design.
- Specify the exact number of distinct item types, any variants, the total placed population and per-type quantities for this first batch. Distinguish types from repeated instances; the current 96-object field and full-game 20–30 ordinary-type target do not automatically determine this batch's count.
- Give each item a stable content ID, display name, dimensions, silhouette, materials/colors and identifying details, size/slot classification, initial sale value, placement/orientation/clearance and burial/depth tendencies. All three are minor/common and **detector-ineligible without exception**, irrespective of size, metal or clusters. State partial-exposure recognition requirements; do not depend on a signal to make the items noticeable.
- Apply the [Steam audit's opening/identity test](../../research/steam-review-audit/discovery-and-payoff.md) at the common tier: recognizable partial shapes, satisfying repeated uncovering/collection and useful baseline income. Do not demand a memorable special moment from every ordinary pickup or put a toy car into this batch to force novelty. State what `09`/`15` should observe; later content owns higher-tier surprise and full-opening variety.
- Preserve held pickup, current 40% exposure plus actual visibility/range, ordinary inventory/sale behavior and durable identities. Describe compatibility when replacing development content in existing saves; do not silently reset or duplicate finds.
- Prepare feasible Blender or free commercial-use source briefs and references. Coordinate appearance with [105's iterative style trials](105-art-style-texture-trials.md); object selection/specification can proceed before final texture selection. Item selection is design approval only; specific assets, files, previews/licenses and removal plans still require approval before creation/import.
- This task owns the starter item selection formerly bundled into `57`. Presentation `57` consumes the accepted set; `40` counts it once and plans the remaining full-game roster. Large/distinctive finds, passive rewards and the final object remain with their existing tasks.
