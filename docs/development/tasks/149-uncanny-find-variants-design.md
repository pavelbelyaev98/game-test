# Task 149 - Design rare uncanny variants of familiar finds

Type: design/research. Status: `planned`. Prerequisites: `117` purpose/frequency/reward decision; use `40`'s candidate roster without waiting for bulk models.

Feature: [discovery content](../../features/backlog/discovery-content.md). Delivery: `150`. Context: [feedback assessment](../../research/excavation-distinctiveness.md).

## Concrete decision artifact

- Compare no variants with a small authored variant set. Prepare five paired briefs: familiar base, unmistakable unusual form/material/context, first-reveal reading, mystery role and why it remains interesting on repeats. A stone tire is illustrative, not a selected asset.
- Define authored type versus appearance versus saved instance; whether a variant shares sale rules/first-discovery record or is a separate discovery. Consume `117`; do not quietly multiply unique counts, income, photos or achievements.
- Compare a roughly 5–10% test occurrence with lower/later alternatives and bounded per-save allocation. Values are hypotheses; seed and persist the selected result rather than rolling on reveal/reload.
- Keep ordinary entries detector-silent even when a variant looks strange. Any proposed non-minor reclassification must be explicit in `40` under the existing detector contract; no value/rarity ping.
- Fit grounded → suspicious → impossible pacing without announcing every joke, making ordinary finds disposable or selecting the final mystery object. Count reused/variant assets within the roster budget and list exact future asset briefs/removal ownership.

## Review and acceptance

Present the paired examples, copy/record/economy table, spawn proposal, estimated art work and rejection criteria. Review theme, surprise frequency and base/variant identity with the user; no new art is approved here.
