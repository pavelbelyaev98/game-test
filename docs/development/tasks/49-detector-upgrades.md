# Task 49 - Paid detector progression

Type: implementation. Status: `planned`. Prerequisites: 48, 10, 56.

Feature: [detector](../../features/backlog/detector.md). [Queue](../tasks.md). [Research basis](../../research/player-review-findings.md).

## Task contract

- After `10`, `35`, `25`, `45` and `48`, add sequential money-purchased detector levels using the existing shop/save boundary. Range and interpretation can improve without identity/value/rarity disclosure, exact distance or GPS targeting.
- Before implementation: read [progression findings](../../research/player-review-findings.md#progression-and-discovery), test searching in real lateral/multi-level layouts and compare headphones/speakers. Implement the level/interpretation milestones accepted in [56](56-progression-design.md); obtain explicit approval for any actual new audio/visual assets. Do not decide a new directional mechanic while wiring the upgrade.
- Show a concise current-to-next effect and its practical benefit. Wider range must not turn dense finds into constant noise or make the entire site beep at once; preserve one foregrounded nearby target, stable switching and quiet gaps. Physical eligibility remains independent from value/rarity at every tier; upgrades do not become a high-value filter.
- Recheck `10`'s suggestive-signal contract at larger ranges: players can ignore a signal to shape routes/chambers. Stronger guidance must not turn the finite site into a waypoint checklist.
- Acceptance: real purchases, overlapping targets, collected/out-of-range silence, menus/focus, save/reload and early/late searching behave correctly. Verify that players can explain the improvement without learning hidden target metadata; inspect the Windows build. Passive additions (`36`) compose with this owned state.
