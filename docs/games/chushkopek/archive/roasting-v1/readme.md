# Chushkopek Season: Just a Few Peppers

Design proposal · researched September 5, 2026 · documentation only

**An outdoor Bulgarian family-workday game: turn Grandpa's suspiciously large pepper supply into a beautiful winter pantry, learning a comfortable rhythm as your little yard operation improves.**

The strongest direction is a small processing game in the family of Cash Cleaner Simulator. Roasting judgment is the proposed main skill; moving batches, opening steaming pots, peeling briefly, packing jars, and filling shelves provide the physical rewards. Grandpa supplies affectionate understatement. The player supplies the competence.

This package develops a complete candidate game, from opening task to ending. It is ready to guide the next design experiments. Enjoyment, audience demand, and achievable playtime remain untested; documentation cannot establish those results.

## Recommended decisions

| Question | Proposed answer |
| --- | --- |
| Where does the work happen? | Entirely outdoors in a backyard beside the street gate. The roaster stays on a dry, open-sided work terrace. |
| What is the main pleasure? | Recognizing a good roast and keeping a small batch moving, followed by tangible finished food. |
| What happens to the peppers? | First a lunch plate; then jars of roasted peppers; later one simple lyutenitsa recipe. |
| How demanding is it? | Relaxed attention, generous readiness windows, no customer deadlines. Players choose how many sockets to use. |
| How does it scale? | Better capacity, fewer repetitive transfers, modest specification changes, and richer finished displays. |
| What is the main objective? | Complete a finite family winter-food list and pack the last family box. |
| What drives the story? | Grandpa has underestimated the work and quietly arranged food for more people than he first mentioned. |
| What is the reward? | A finished pantry, packed family gifts, recognition of the player's competence, and a small cellar surprise. |
| Is peeling the centerpiece? | No longer the default assumption. Keep a short tactile peel; compare it against batch assistance. |
| Is this a factory or restaurant? | A compact household operation with eight authored main jobs. |

## Three moments the game should earn

1. **The first reveal:** blackened skin slips away to expose glossy red flesh. The player understands why roasting was worthwhile.
2. **The first comfortable batch:** three peppers finish at staggered times, a covered bowl rests, and a jar fills without frantic clicking. The player feels more capable.
3. **The last crate:** the same yard that held an intimidating pile now holds ordered food and family parcels. Grandpa finally admits there were quite a few peppers.

These are design targets. Each has a corresponding playtest in [Scope and validation](scope-and-validation.md).
