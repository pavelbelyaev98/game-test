# Idea at a glance

First-person excavation game: progress from a simple shovel to absurd digging capability.

**Loop:** dig → detect → uncover → collect → return → sell → upgrade → repeat.

## Current direction

- Preserve the liked normal Scoop digging; design more distinctive earned progression and memorable discoveries. Excavation guns are wanted with implementation deferred; rapid Shave is liked, while the other trial modes remain an admin-only experimental package.
- Open content decisions: unique-find purpose and retention, the first discovery roster brief, handling for large/special finds, and early encounter pacing. A validated slice gates bulk content production.
- Audio direction: ambient nature plus digging/action feedback; each specific sound needs approval. The user owns terrain art; do not add unrelated scenery or props.

## Core constraints

- Digging and discovery dominate play time; surface visits are short checkpoints. Starting battery/inventory already support a satisfying expedition.
- Allow downward, diagonal and meaningful sideways excavation without pre-dug routes; expand variety through material areas. Caves and pre-existing tunnel/chamber areas are not planned. A straight shaft must not be overwhelmingly more profitable.
- Validate random layouts for novelty gaps and clumping before accepting and persisting a population. The passive detector suggests one eligible non-minor find and never reveals identity or rarity; **all minor/common finds are silent**.
- Keep controls and progression clear and menus light. Camera comfort and held precision movement stay respected by stronger equipment; the 32 m site stays readable and visibility adds no battery drain or placement chore.
- Avoid puzzle gating, identification chores, multiple currencies and mandatory item-by-item carrying. Optional RMB lift/drop/throw is selected.
- Start grounded and become increasingly strange and absurd. Rare uncanny variants and optional timelapse/camp ideas remain deferred proposals; unique purpose and retention are undecided.
- Target a dense 2–3 hour first completion with useful purchases and recognizable finds into late play. The shaped excavation is part of the reward; completion concerns discoveries, not deleting every voxel.
- Preserve the excavation across periodic and transaction saves, rescue and ending/Continue. Default digging is click-and-hold with an optional Toggle mode; the rare permanent rewards are optional.
