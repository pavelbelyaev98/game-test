## Work

- Extend the same bench with one clearly described powered-conversion purchase after the two-offer prototype has passed its gate. Record price, any visible equipment prerequisites, finite affordability and repeated work remaining; no hidden access, component hunt or food threshold followed by payment.
- Author a substantial powered mechanism/material movement that retains meaningful direct operation and reduces useful operator or output work. Initial input/output reference is 96 units with the same 48-unit carrier, combining two loads or collecting early partial output.
- Preserve one station/input/output, reusable carrier and broad handoff, both earlier purchase benefits and all loads. Install at a safe cycle boundary with paid ownership intact. A local feeder extension is optional; distant-intake relocation and substantial route shortening are no longer requirements.
- Retain the complete price/effect/status/location card and [owned attachment lifecycle](../state-and-saving.md#attachment-ownership-and-installation). The powered purchase includes its complete equipment; no separate kit shopping or long haul. A few chunky authored modules are only a later candidate if the prototype snap proved worthwhile, not mandatory construction scope. Drawing remains an alternative presentation candidate, never an extra required assembly step.
- Compare modified versus powered apparatus on the same 96-unit job/work area/carrier. Record strokes/actions, held effort, transfers, output collections, travel, waits and total work with repeated trials and actual physical observations.
- Test both earlier purchase orders, combined improvements, repeated/unaffordable requests, paid pending installation/save restore, partial final output and completion without buying every offer.

## Acceptance

- The conversion changes useful operation/material handling and improves the complete job beyond trial variability. A bigger model, capacity number or idle timer alone does not pass.
- Finite budget and purchase timing permit repeated useful use without extra supply or a required clearing path. Earlier improvements remain effective and neither purchase order strands progress.
- Loaded/pending installation, saved Coins/ownership, one active set of targets, reservations and exact food remain valid.

## Human playtest check

Compare the same batch before/after buying the conversion. Operate it repeatedly, collect two loads together or a partial output, and restore a paid pending installation. Explain what the machinery actually improved.
