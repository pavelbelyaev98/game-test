# 2_02 — Upgrade throughput and handling

Milestone: M2 · Type: Feature · Status: [central task queue](readme.md#ordered-task-queue)

**Outcome:** Prove that each first purchase and their combination improve the physical food job and offer a useful choice.

**Depends on:** [2_01 — Coins and two equipment improvements](2_01_wheelbarrow-discovery-and-loader.md). All earlier play gates must be resolved under the queue rules.

## Work

- Compare the same finite job (initially 48 units) with baseline, capacity first, assisted handling first and both combined. Use the same source/work area/handoff and equivalent starting food state; record configuration and any genuine layout differences.
- Measure gather/staging, travel, pour, operator strokes/held time, internal waiting, output-grouping effort, output trips, handoff and empty return. Use the same bulk-handling method for matched throughput trials; record optional individual handling separately so its chosen pace is not treated as a baseline defect. Record the material/operation change alongside elapsed time, not just capacity. Never slow the enjoyable baseline or add a long walk to sell relief.
- Report one-time purchase/installation separately from recurring cycles: finding/understanding the complete offer and mount, placements/rejections, fitting time and any safe-boundary wait. Then compare handling/travel and useful work remaining after installation for either first purchase. Keep kit/instructions at the same workstation; compulsory individual pepper/jar/component errands are a failure to remove, not a source of upgrade value. Preserve optional single pickup, predictable bulk pickup and the tested small output-grouping payoff; an improvement must not silently automate away every satisfying action. Do not add timers or mandatory supply to improve the gathering-to-service ratio.
- Tune the two useful effects, coordinated buffers and prices from observed bottlenecks. Demonstrate an early purchase, repeated use, and a finite total that permits both useful orders without stranding later equipment.
- Record choice prompts neutrally, actual player observations when provided, and any weak or redundant offer. Fix an offer that changes nothing useful before the gate; do not add a third prototype purchase to avoid the comparison.

## Acceptance

- Reproducible evidence covers baseline, each offer and the combination through stored food, with no accounting or affordability blockers.
- Evidence distinguishes the one-time installation cost from recurring improvement, including observed confusion/rejected placements and the next appropriate batch's gain. Offer cards communicate full price, workflow effect, lifecycle status and mount without a remembered shopping list. Report optional gag liking separately; it cannot rescue a weak effect.
- Each first purchase changes actual batch handling/operation usefully; combined effects retain prior benefits. Larger numbers, shorter idle timers without a bottleneck, and spectacle alone do not pass.
- The delivered comparison build includes earning/spending, exact test costs/effects and enough finite supply for repeated improvement use. Missing human preference is explicit.

## Human playtest check

Try each offer first on comparable work. Describe why you chose it, how you understood the loading kit/mount, what changed during the batch, and which operation you wanted to repeat after installation. Record placement attempts and recurring work separately.

**Outside this task:** extra mandatory supply, forced wait padding, more shops, story used to rescue weak handling or full-yard art.
