# 4_01 — Open graybox yard and work area

Milestone: M4 · Type: Feature · Status: [central task queue](readme.md#ordered-task-queue)

**Outcome:** Expand the proven food job into a compact mostly accessible yard with comfortable staging and working routes.

**Depends on:** [3_03 — Save failure recovery](3_03_save-failure-recovery.md). All earlier play gates must be resolved under the queue rules.

## Work

- Build a compact outdoor work area with supplies, apparatus, upgrade bench, broad handoff and storage view reachable from the start. Keep most of the yard walkable; do not recreate mandatory blocked passages, buried tools or the former A–E five-pocket progression.
- Stage finite source groups near useful work positions and author stable IDs/total. Preserve local depletion and free carrier/prop placement, rotation, stacking and recovery; using supply makes space without an area-clear objective.
- Provide short, optional route choices and shed/cellar/street stand-ins. Movable clutter can be moved again; fixed boundaries/fixtures are physically readable. No locked cellar approach or compulsory shortcut.
- Carry forward the authorized Coins bench, both improvement combinations and direct machine operation. Reserve space for the powered conversion at this workstation rather than a distant-supply reveal. Record content/save compatibility and measured walking/staging.

## Acceptance

- All finite supplies, essential work stations, bench and handoff are accessible with baseline equipment; no discovery quota or hidden access state is needed.
- Both purchase orders, chosen staging/prop arrangements and save/reload preserve food, Coins, capabilities and usable routes. Supply never expands unexpectedly.
- The layout supports the food job without unnecessary walking or areas added to match the old map.

## Human playtest check

Walk the yard before preparing food, inspect the bench/storage, stage loads where you choose, move clutter and complete batches using both purchase orders. Report any forced path or needless haul.

**Outside this task:** extra levels, cooking interiors, blocked-yard campaign, factory construction, new economy systems or second activities.
