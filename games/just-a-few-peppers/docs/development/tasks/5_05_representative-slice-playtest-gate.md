## Acceptance

- The actual slice build completes with recorded rules/integration/save/performance evidence and no unresolved blocker introduced by presentation.

This is a review gate. Prepare the playable/reviewable artifact first; keep missing human evidence pending and do not silently advance beyond the gate.

**Outside this task:** Broad asset production before this decision, inferred fun from screenshots, or replaying all unrelated checks without cause.
