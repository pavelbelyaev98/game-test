**Depends on:** [1_06 — Loose yard objects](1_06_loose-yard-objects-and-playful-handling.md). All earlier play gates must be resolved under the queue rules.

## Work

- Reuse the completed crate/handoff loop and shared physical props. Compare grouped/moving representation against a manageable batch of physical peppers during pouring/loading in the same small work area; use the same quantities and timing conditions.
- Let physical peppers contact the carrier/intake and settle convincingly. Choose active-body limits, sleeping/reuse and grouped resting contents from measured behavior rather than a blanket no-physics rule. Use placeholders or suitable licensed assets; no full-harvest simulation requirement.
- Record whether bodies are a physical view of a committed batch or individually registered recoverable material. If food can leave a container as authoritative loose material, give its units one stable owner, conservation term and recovery route before allowing it; no silent loss or second copy. Do not require collecting tiny strays to finish.
- Verify pause/focus, interrupted pours, off-target drops, body recovery, repeated loads and partial final amounts in the actual scene/player. Record active/sleeping body counts, frame behavior, visual contact and limitations.
- Choose and integrate a technically viable default, retain useful comparison evidence and report pending human preference honestly. No fun rating is inferred from physics or screenshots.

## Acceptance

- The chosen representation visibly moves material through contact while preserving exact units through full/partial pours and recovery.
- Packaged input checks and observed contacts cover repeated use, physical interruption and the final partial batch. No routine tunnelling, growing body leak or unrecoverable food remains.
- The decision states what is simulated, what owns food, measured limits, tradeoffs and untested feel. It does not ban important physical objects or claim every pepper must be simulated.

## Human playtest check

Pour a full and partial load, inspect the edge/contact, miss the intake or drop a carrier, pause during motion, then recover and finish the same food. Compare the material feel with the recorded alternative.

**Outside this task:** new machine operations (1_08), Coins, all-yard pepper rigidbodies, cooking judgment, disk saves or second products.
