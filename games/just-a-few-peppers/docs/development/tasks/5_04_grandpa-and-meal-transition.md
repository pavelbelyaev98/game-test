## Work

- Place one seated Grandpa with limited gestures and a small selection from the canonical English opening/discovery/ending draft bank. Use readable subtitles and quiet gaps; optional licensed voice is not a prerequisite for functional dialogue. Preserve eventual localization/native review as later presentation work, without building a localization system here.
- Preserve 4_03's completed yard, natural machine idle, and normal movement/camera control. Do not replace them with a mandatory meal transition.
- Optionally add a simple table/gift visual, short thank-you, or offscreen greeting derived from harvest completion. Keep it cuttable and nonmodal: no meal interaction, cinematic system, additional completion flag, walking family AI, teleport, credits fade, or menu ejection.
- Restore the completed property without replaying gifts or queued remarks; keep required interactions understandable with speech muted.

## Acceptance

- Dialogue never interrupts a transfer, blocks a tool, or invents a jar-return objective. The full game's selection stays within about 24 short lines.
- The final deposit keeps 4_03's harvest-completion behavior. No Finish button, required table visit, meal playback, or dialogue choice. Any table food uses existing household supplies and creates no additional inventory or chores.
- Pausing or restoring the completed property does not undo completion or replay the bottle/gift. Verify the 100% household display, continued control, and optional presentation together.

## Human playtest check

Play a few loads with dialogue muted, make the last deposit, and confirm control remains available. Pause/reload, inspect the completed scene, and verify any optional thank-you/table/gift beat does not replay or gate exit.

**Outside this task:** Mandatory meal playback, cinematic/credits systems, NPC schedules, dialogue choices, a voiced-protagonist system, drinking buffs, distillation, or meal preparation gameplay.
