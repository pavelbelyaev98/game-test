## M1 — prove a load

Resume partial feedback first, currently 1_02, while preserving useful movement and 1_03 automatic processing. Complete free carriers and handoff, then the interim 1_05 loop checkpoint. 1_06 tests loose props, 1_07 compares manageable physical pepper batches and 1_08 makes a handle/rack directly responsive. See the [M1 contract](first-playable-task.md).

Keep one accessible work corner, short useful trips and enough finite material for repeated/partial batches. The representation choice is per object type, not a global physics ban. Primitive mechanisms/props are acceptable if the actual action can be judged. No buried tool objective, household scenes, final conversion, second food activity or disk saves.

## M2 — prove earning, choice and useful machinery

2_01 adds proportional Coins at handoff, one nearby bench with both offers visible and independently useful capacity/handling improvements. The wheelbarrow can belong to coordinated capacity; it is purchased equipment rather than a buried discovery. The second attachment changes operator work and stays useful at either capacity. Food milestones are presentation, never an extra threshold before payment.

Then 2_01 presents the assisted-loading purchase through [one nearby complete kit and forgiving mount](../core-loop-and-mechanics.md#attach-a-purchased-improvement), preserving paid entitlement and active food. The capacity package adds no assembly action. Cards contain full price/effect/status/location; no separate shopping or hauling. At most one inexpensive optional static gag may accompany this test without a computer/mail system.

2_02 compares baseline, each first purchase and both on the same finite food job. Measure strokes/held effort, transfers, travel, waiting, output handling, stored units and purchase timing. Test affordability and repeated use in either order. Do not make baseline controls annoying to sell the solution.

Separate one-time finding/understanding/fitting time and rejected placements from recurring batch costs. M3 extends the existing save tasks to loose, held, recovered, fitted-pending and installed attachment states; disk persistence does not move into M2.

2_03 is the human gate for the whole revised prototype, including earning/spending. Use the scope's nine questions and six-player approach where practical; explicit smaller-sample acceptance retains uncertainty. Missing observations remain pending. Retain at most two bounded feel revisions before revisiting failure; more content does not rescue weak machinery. Coins are already authorized and cannot be postponed past this gate.

## M3 — make progress dependable

Implement the [state contract](state-and-saving.md): exact food and optional registered loose material, free poses/stacks, mechanism phase, budget, paid/installed upgrades and pending installation. Save committed coherent state; reload cannot re-award a handoff or charge twice. Validate schema/content, recover invalid poses and keep one valid backup on write failure. No cloud, slot browser or Stage0-save compatibility is required.

## M4 — complete the functional game

Use the smallest open-yard work layout that supports the proven food job; the old A–E/five-pocket/732-unit map is historical. Most of the property, supplies, bench, handoff and storage view are accessible initially. Supply use may reveal space but not mandatory tool access.

Add Grandpa's powered conversion through the same bench, with clear price/equipment requirements and repeated food work remaining. Test distinct useful operation/material movement and whole-job gains on the same 96-unit reference job; a required distant intake and shorter final-supply route are retired. Preserve both earlier purchase benefits and exact ownership through pending installation/load.

Final valid handoff stores the last food, earns its proportional Coins and completes winter preparation once. Keep normal control and stockpile views; yard neatness, remaining budget and unbought equipment add no conditions.

## M5–M6 — earn and finish the presentation

Build a representative short arc with real material contact, directly operated machinery, purchase effects, finished-food handoff/displays and Grandpa before dressing the whole setting. Profile the actual assets and save/UI/physics behavior, then obtain the 5_05 human decision. Follow [asset policy](unity-and-assets.md); record actual imports/licenses and keep shared controls and recoverable IDs.

Finish the compact yard using consistent movable/fixed affordances, useful staging surfaces and readable apparatus. Choose dialogue for batch work, purchases/installations and winter food; retired hidden-wheel/path triggers are not restored. Measure duration and cut empty walks or excessive supply. Optional props and closing presentation do not become chores.

5_04/6_02 select restrained original comic variety within the existing writing budget, with later rereading, clear instructions and no notification replay after load. Drawing-to-attachment presentation, a few chunky hero modules and atmospheric lighting remain conditional later candidates, not extra tasks. No stress, compulsory rest or day-management progression is added.

## M7–M9 — finish the product

Complete operation/grab/pour/bench bindings, sensitivity/FOV, audio/display and guidance. State the difference between stored food and spendable budget. Verify partial last work, both purchase orders, duplicate/failed transactions, physics recovery, saves and the completed yard. Profile active bodies and meaningful mechanism/material motion instead of silently disabling it.

Prepare truthful clips of physical handling, direct operation, chosen equipment effects and the growing winter stockpile. Record hardware/duration only from evidence. Keep versioned artifacts/credits and exact candidate checks. Publishing/contacting others requires authorization.

## How agents take work

Read [AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md), the [queue](tasks/readme.md), one complete selected brief, its linked contracts and dependency delivery records. Implement one task, own all wiring, verify changed behavior and record an honest handoff. Do not restart delivered groundwork or treat planning changes as player acceptance.

## Future activity decisions

After pepper machinery is proven, consider at most one compact lyutenitsa or rakia activity only through a separate scoped decision and task. Inputs/output, distinct physical payoff, saves and completion implications must be concrete first. Neither is permanent forbidden scenery nor an authorized extra production chain now.
