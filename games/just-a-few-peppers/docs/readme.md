# Just a few peppers

Current processing-and-inventions direction · September 6, 2026 · [task readiness and feedback](development/tasks/readme.md)

**Help Grandpa prepare an unreasonable amount of winter food using increasingly absurd homemade machinery.** Physically handle useful batches, directly operate the apparatus, see finished food accumulate, earn Coins and choose equipment improvements that make the next batch better.

The yard is a compact outdoor Bulgarian setting, mostly walkable from the beginning. Preparing food is the goal. Equipment choices provide progression. Supplies diminish as they are used; mandatory passage clearing and hidden-equipment discovery are no longer the campaign structure.

The prototype loop is **HANDLE A BATCH → LOAD/OPERATE → FINISHED WINTER FOOD → HAND OFF → EARN COINS → CHOOSE AN IMPROVEMENT → USE IT REPEATEDLY**. Internal cooking/preparation stays compressed; there is no ten-step routine, burn judgment, fuel, jams or repairs. One generous handoff permanently stores food and returns the empty finished carrier automatically. Winter food and spendable Coins have separate readouts and ownership.

The [latest targeted refinement](design-pivot.md#snap-installation-and-restrained-comic-variety--september-6-2026) keeps that direction and adds [one complete attachment snap](core-loop-and-mechanics.md#attach-a-purchased-improvement) to the existing assisted-loading purchase in 2_01. The nearby bench shows each full price, workflow effect, installation state and location. [Optional comic variety](jobs-events-and-comedy.md#optional-messages-and-comic-variety) stays sparse; drawing and atmospheric lighting remain later candidates.

## Current design state snapshot

- **Required now:** one finite pepper food job, a freely handled batch/finished carrier, directly responsive apparatus, one broad handoff, Coins and two useful purchases at one visible bench before the prototype gate, with one short assisted-loading snap installation and reliable paid-kit recovery.
- **Physics:** use it where interaction benefits. Important gameplay objects may be physical. Test manageable physical peppers and grouped representations; conserve/recover the same contents and choose per object type.
- **Progression:** earning, choosing and using improvements. Stockpile milestones drive presentation only; no automatic food threshold plus payment, buried tools or yard locks.
- **References:** Cash Cleaner for physical handling/processing, Food Processing for equipment progression, the garden-invention game for absurd domestic escalation. Leaf it Alone is secondary for upgrades/pacing/comfort, not the map template.
- **Future decisions:** lyutenitsa or rakia may justify one compact additional activity after pepper machinery is proven. No second activity is authorized now and neither is permanently forbidden.
- **Optional presentation:** at most one inexpensive static gag may accompany the prototype upgrade test. Later messages share the existing writing budget; drawing/lighting are candidates, not required tasks, and add no stress or day-management systems.
- **Actual evidence:** movement, scooping and automatic processing exist. Free placement still needs the 1_02 correction; usable finished output is 1_04, physical batch comparison 1_07, direct operation 1_08 and Coins/purchases 2_01.

## First-version limits

One outdoor yard, one pepper apparatus with useful conversions, one current product, one handoff, one equipment budget and a small set of meaningful improvements. All essential work locations are accessible. Keep shared movable props, English draft text and a seated Grandpa; household displays read stored food without inventories or errands. No customers, food sales, recurring bills, big tree, factory expansion, parts hunt or chore systems.

## Three moments to earn

1. A useful batch tumbles into the feeder and your input drives a substantial mechanism.
2. You buy an improvement and the next load visibly moves or operates better.
3. Grandpa's excessive apparatus handles substantial winter-food batches; the final handoff leaves the stockpile complete and normal yard control available.

These are targets, not proof of fun. Ordinary handling and machinery must be enjoyable between purchases and jokes.
